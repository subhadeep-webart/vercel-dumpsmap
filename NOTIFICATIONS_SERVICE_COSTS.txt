================================================================================
DUMPSMAP - NOTIFICATIONS: THIRD-PARTY SERVICE COSTS
================================================================================

Subject : What the notification feature will cost to RUN each month
Note    : This document covers third-party service fees only. Development

All prices verified against official vendor pricing pages in August 2026.
Sources are listed at the end of this document.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE SHORT ANSWER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Push notifications are FREE. There is no per-message charge, at any volume.

  Push notifications to phone/desktop ............ $0 per month
  In-app notifications (bell + page) ............. $0 per month
  Email notifications ............................ $0 to $20 per month

The only service that ever costs money is email, and only once volume grows
past the free allowance. At DumpsMap's current scale that is very likely $0.

No credit card is required to start. No Apple Developer account is required.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PUSH NOTIFICATIONS - $0 PER MONTH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

There are two ways to send push notifications. Both are free.

  OPTION A - FIREBASE CLOUD MESSAGING (FCM)
  Cost: $0. Unlimited messages.

    Google lists Cloud Messaging as "No-cost" on every Firebase plan. There
    is no per-message fee, no monthly cap, and no overage charge. This is
    true at 5,000 messages a month and at 5,000,000.

    No credit card is required. A billing account is only needed if you use
    other Firebase products (database, file storage, cloud functions), which
    this feature does not.

  OPTION B - SELF-HOSTED WEB PUSH  [OUR RECOMMENDATION]
  Cost: $0. No account with anyone.

    The browser push services run by Google, Mozilla and Apple are free to
    use and require no registration or account at all. The security keys are
    generated on our own server at no cost.

    This runs entirely on DumpsMap's existing server and database. It adds
    no new vendor, no new bill, and no dependency on Google.

  WHY WE RECOMMEND OPTION B
  Both cost nothing, but Option A ties DumpsMap to a Google service that
  could change its terms later. Option B has no third party involved at all,
  so there is no account that can be suspended and no pricing that can
  change. It also fits the existing codebase more naturally.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. EMAIL NOTIFICATIONS - $0 TO $20 PER MONTH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This is the only line item that can cost money. Email needs a sending service
so that messages land in inboxes rather than spam folders.

  RECOMMENDED: RESEND

    Free tier    : 3,000 emails per month (limit of 100 per day)
    Paid tier    : $20 per month for 50,000 emails
    Credit card  : Not required for the free tier

    At DumpsMap's current volume the free tier is very likely enough. The
    one thing to watch is the 100-per-day limit: 3,000 a month averages 100
    a day exactly, so a busy day can hit the ceiling even when the monthly
    total is fine. If sending is uneven, budget $20 per month.

  CHEAPEST AT HIGH VOLUME: AMAZON SES

    Cost         : $0.10 per 1,000 emails
    Credit card  : Required (AWS account)

    Roughly four times cheaper than Resend at volume, but more work to set
    up and requires approval from Amazon before it can send to real users.
    Worth switching to only if email volume passes about 100,000 a month.

  A WARNING ABOUT SENDGRID

    SendGrid discontinued its free plan on 27 May 2025. It is now a 60-day
    trial, after which sending stops until you pay $19.95 per month. If you
    have seen older advice recommending SendGrid's free tier, that advice is
    now out of date.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SMS NOTIFICATIONS - NOT RECOMMENDED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SMS is included here for completeness, but we advise against it for general
notifications.

  Cost per message : approx. $0.012 to $0.013 (Twilio, including carrier fees)
  Phone number     : $1.15 per month
  Registration     : One-time and recurring US carrier fees also apply

  WHY WE ADVISE AGAINST IT
  SMS costs roughly 30 to 60 times more than email, and infinitely more than
  push, which is free. At 50,000 users receiving 10 messages a month, SMS
  would cost about $6,500 per month, where push costs nothing at all.

  If SMS is ever wanted, restrict it to critical events only, such as login
  codes. Never use it for general notifications.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. TOTAL MONTHLY COST AS DUMPSMAP GROWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Assuming each user receives about 10 notifications per month.

  PUSH NOTIFICATIONS ONLY (recommended starting point)

      500 users  ........  $0 per month
    5,000 users  ........  $0 per month
   50,000 users  ........  $0 per month

    Free at every scale. There is no volume at which push starts to cost
    money.

  PUSH + EMAIL (email used as a fallback for about half the notifications)

      500 users  ........  $0 per month        (within the free allowance)
    5,000 users  ........  $0 to $20 per month (Resend free tier or paid)
   50,000 users  ........  $25 to $350 per month

    At 50,000 users the range depends on the service: about $25 with Amazon
    SES, about $350 with Resend. That decision can be made later, when the
    volume actually arrives.

  PUSH + EMAIL + SMS (SMS for critical alerts only)

    Add approximately $2 / $14 / $131 per month at the three scales above.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THINGS THAT ARE OFTEN ASSUMED TO COST MONEY, BUT DO NOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  APPLE DEVELOPER ACCOUNT - NOT REQUIRED
    Push notifications on iPhone do not require the $99 per year Apple
    Developer Program. Apple's own developer documentation confirms this.
    That fee applies only to publishing a native app to the App Store, which
    this feature does not involve.

  GOOGLE CLOUD / FIREBASE BILLING - NOT REQUIRED
    Firebase Cloud Messaging works without a billing account or credit card.

  PER-MESSAGE PUSH FEES - DO NOT EXIST
    Neither Firebase nor self-hosted web push charges per notification sent,
    at any volume.

  ONE GENUINE LIMITATION TO BE AWARE OF
    On iPhone, users must add DumpsMap to their home screen before push
    notifications can reach them. This is an Apple restriction that applies
    to every website, not something specific to DumpsMap. It costs nothing,
    but it does mean fewer iPhone users will opt in than Android users.
    Android and desktop have no such requirement.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Notifications are effectively free to run.

Push costs nothing at any scale. Email is free up to 3,000 messages a month,
and $20 a month after that. There are no setup fees, no minimum commitments,
and no Apple or Google charges.

For DumpsMap today, the realistic ongoing cost is $0 per month, rising to
around $20 per month if and when email volume grows past the free allowance.
